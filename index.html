About ZuMark Pharma

ZuMark Pharma is a trusted and rapidly growing allopathic pharmaceutical company dedicated to making high-quality, safe, and affordable healthcare accessible to every family. Specializing in the Dermatology and General Physician segments, we deliver clinically reliable, result-oriented formulations designed to meet everyday therapeutic needs without compromising on purity or safety.

Operating from our corporate base in the Mumbai region, we ensure that every product undergoes stringent quality control checks. Our commitment lies in building lasting trust with medical practitioners, chemists, and patients across India through ethical practices and continuous innovation.

Founder & Director

Driving Excellence in Allopathic Healthcare

“Quality healthcare should never be a privilege—it must be accessible, reliable, and affordable for every family.”

As the Founder and Director of ZuMark Pharma, my mission is anchored in the belief that effective medical treatment should be within reach of every individual. With a deep understanding of the pharmaceutical landscape, I established ZuMark Pharma to bridge the gap between premium clinical formulations and everyday affordability.

We focus intensely on Dermatology and General Physician care, ensuring that each product—from advanced creams to essential therapeutics—delivers tangible results. Operating from our headquarters in the Mumbai region, we maintain rigorous oversight of our distribution and quality standards, ensuring complete reliability for our partners and patients.

Looking ahead, we are expanding our portfolio to include critical antibiotics and respiratory care, reinforcing our dedication to comprehensive community health.
