About ZuMark Pharma

ZuMark Pharma is a growing allopathic pharmaceutical company working in the Dermatology and General Physician segments. We aim to make safe, affordable healthcare available to every family.

Every product goes through quality control checks. We build trust with medical practitioners, chemists and patients across India through ethical practice and continuous improvement.

Our work centres on two areas: Dermatology, where we develop creams, ointments and cleansing products for common skin concerns, and General Physician care, where we are preparing antibiotic tablets and cough syrups for everyday family health needs.

Operating from our corporate base in the Mumbai region, we keep close oversight of our manufacturing partners, packaging and distribution. Our aim is simple: clinically reliable formulations, clear labelling, and prices that families can afford.

We work with doctors, chemists and distributors who share this goal, and we welcome feedback from medical practitioners so that our range keeps getting better.

Founder & Director

Driving excellence in allopathic healthcare
“Quality healthcare should never be a privilege—it must be accessible, reliable, and affordable for every family.”

As the Founder and Director of ZuMark Pharma, my mission is anchored in the belief that effective medical treatment should be within reach of every individual. Looking ahead, we are expanding our portfolio to include antibiotics and respiratory care, reinforcing our dedication to comprehensive community health.
