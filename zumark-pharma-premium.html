About ZUMARK PHARMA

ZUMARK PHARMA is a Mumbai based pharmaceutical company with its office in Nalasopara East. We are a young, fast growing company with one simple vision: A New Vision for Better Health.

Everything we put on a pack is meant to be clear. The product name is bold, the salt composition is easy to read, and the red Z capsule logo sits in the same place every time. Doctors, chemists and partners can see at a glance what a product is and who makes it.

Our MELA MARK range is the first chapter of the ZUMARK PHARMA story, and new brands are coming soon.
